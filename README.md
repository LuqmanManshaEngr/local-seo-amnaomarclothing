**Reporting period:** Jan 1 - Aug 3, 2026 (year-to-date snapshot)

### The story in the numbers

Over this period the site logged **398 sessions from 252 active users**, with Direct traffic still dominating the mix at 85.9%. Organic Search is a small slice of
volume; 44 sessions, 11.1% of the total; but it is the **highest-quality traffic source on the site**
- **68.18% engagement rate** on Organic sessions vs. **37.44% site-wide** (1.8x)
- **26.1 seconds average engagement time** vs. 16.2 seconds for Direct
- **6.57 events per session** vs. 5.89 site-wide
- Organic Search brought in **33 new users (13.15%** of all new users) despite being only 11% of sessions; punching above its weight on acquisition

In plain terms: the people who find this store through Google stay longer, interact more, and convert into new users at a better rate than the traffic the site already
gets for free through direct/bookmarked visits. That is the classic early-stage SEO signal; small volume, strong intent.

### What this doesn't claim

This is a single-period snapshot, not a growth trend. The GA4 exports available for this write-up report one aggregated total for Jan-Aug 2026, there is no monthly
breakdown in the data, so no month-over-month growth percentage is shown here. Once monthly-granularity data is pulled, this section will be updated with an actual
trend line rather than an inferred one.

### Where the traffic is coming from

Geographically the audience skews toward the **United States (40.5%)** and **Pakistan (19.1%)**, with smaller but active segments in Poland, the UK, and India; a mix
that reflects both the brand's paid/organic reach and its existing customer base. Desktop still leads the device mix at 64.2%, with mobile at 34.2%, which matters for
how content and product pages are prioritized for indexing and Core Web Vitals.

### Recommendations this data points to

1. **Scale Organic Search volume, not just quality.** The engagement numbers already prove the content resonates with people who arrive via Google; the gap is
impressions and clicks, not page quality. Expanding collection-level and product-level content (sizing detail, fabric notes, styling guidance) written with genuine
first-hand product knowledge aligns with Google's tightened 2026 emphasis on demonstrated expertise and experience (E-E-A-T) in its March 2026 core update.
2. **Close the ecommerce funnel gap.** The Drive Sales funnel currently shows 0 completed purchases against 3 add-to-carts in this period; a conversion-path issue
(checkout friction, trust signals, or page speed), worth auditing before pushing more top-of-funnel Organic traffic at it.
3. **Mobile experience as a ranking lever, not just a UX nicety.** With over a third of active users on mobile, page experience there directly supports the "helpful
content generally comes with good page experience" signal Google continues to reinforce.

### Methodology

All figures are pulled directly from the GA4 property for `amnaomarclothing.com`, covering Jan 1 - Aug 3, 2026, exported 2026-08-03.
"Organic Search" refers to GA4's default channel grouping (Session/First user primary channel group = Organic Search). No figures in this report or the accompanying
graphic are estimated, projected, or interpolated; every number traces back to a GA4 export.
