## Executive Overview

Between **March 1 and November 16, 2026**, I executed a targeted **Local SEO, product catalog schema, on-page collection optimization, and GA4 funnel tracking** strategy for **Amna Omar Clothing**. Rather than chasing low-intent global vanity clicks, the campaign focused on capturing high-intent fashion buyers across major Pakistani metropolitan hubs and key international diaspora markets while establishing early visibility in AI answer engines.

> **Data Integrity & Reporting Methodology:** All figures below come directly from verified GA4 exports (`2026-11-16`). Channel-level performance metrics (`Organic Search`, `Direct`, `Referral`, `AI Assistant`, `Organic Social`) represent **exact March-November aggregate totals** (without synthetic monthly channel interpolation), while the monthly acquisition trajectory reflects **verified site-wide weekly user cohorts** aggregated across GA4 Weeks 9-46 (`401` total new users).

### Strategic Takeaways from Channel Data
* **Superior Search Intent:** While Direct traffic accounted for baseline brand visits.
* **Organic Search delivered nearly double the engagement rate (`65.38%` vs. `36.18%`)**
* Higher interaction depth (`7.42` events/session vs. `6.64`), proving that search visitors landed on relevant collection and product pages aligned with purchase intent.
* **AI Discovery Readiness:** Structured product descriptions and entity clarity enabled `amnaomarclothing.com` to capture.
* **6 sessions and 5 new users directly from `chatgpt.com / ai-assistant`**, demonstrating early Answer Engine & Generative Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO) visibility.

## Local SEO & Geographic Footprint

* **Primary Local Target - Pakistan (`96` Active Users):** Produced **`182` engaged sessions** (`1.90` engaged sessions per active user), a **`62.12%` engagement rate**, **`239.01s` average engagement time**, and **`2,878` events** (`65.0%` of all website events).
* **Top Pakistani Cities:** **Islamabad (`32`)**, **Multan (`31`)**, **Lahore (`13`)**, **Rawalpindi (`13`)**, and **Karachi (`11`)**, supported by secondary regional demand in Sukkur (`4`), Dera Ghazi Khan (`2`), and Faisalabad (`2`).
* **International Diaspora Reach (`23` Countries / `84+` Cities):** Strong engagement from Pakistani fashion diaspora hubs in the **United Kingdom (`18` users, `52.38%` engagement rate)**, **Saudi Arabia (`8` users, `58.33%` engagement rate)**, **United States (`143` users, `31` engaged sessions)**, **Canada (`7`)**, and **Australia (`4`)**.
